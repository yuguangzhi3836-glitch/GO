# C04 → C11 minimum deposit/damage boundary proposal

Not implemented, not authority to operate money. Existing bridge supports rental price capture/refund and RENTAL_CHANGE; neither proves a rental deposit authorization. Current case ends C11_MONEY_REVIEW_REQUIRED. Keep that state until the following chain is implemented and independently verified.

1. Source authority: resolve rental owner, verified supplier/payee, currency, signed/versioned contract liability terms, vehicle binding and explicit customer deposit consent from durable server-side facts. Current rental schema does not contain all those facts; use explicit isolated synthetic fixtures for engineering, keep real operations HOLD. Never take request amount/payee/business_type as authority.
2. Dedicated business identity: add C11-supported RENTAL_DEPOSIT source resolver/FactBinding using rental order and contract revision; create a unique PaymentOrderRoot with an Intent. Bind AUTHORIZATION receipt to this root. Do not reclassify a rental rent capture or RENTAL_CHANGE as a deposit.
3. Case decision: C04 exposes read-only verified adjudication fact (case ID, immutable decision revision/hash, order ID, owner, currency, awarded amount, authorized independent reviewer and source contract). Authentication of a case record is not authenticity of its underlying inspection submissions. Approved policy/evidence intake remains a prerequisite for actual payment instructions.
4. C11 instructions: resolve C04 fact server-side; freeze intended capture/release amounts and deterministic operation keys against the original deposit authorization movement. Root/Intent/Movement/Ledger remain the sole money authority. Partial capture and release must conserve authorized balance under one serialized root: capture + release cannot exceed authorization, and retries cannot allocate again.
5. Outcomes: UNKNOWN external outcome stays pending; recovery queries the original operation identity and reconciles durable receipts. No speculative new payment request. Mark capture/release complete only from validated C11 receipt facts, never provider boolean or a C04 status.
6. Lifecycle: a pending dispute prevents capture instructions; expiration/silence is not consent. A later appeal or reversal creates linked adjudication/compensation facts and uses the existing C11 refund authority. Never mutate history or booked money.
7. Required isolated tests: wrong owner/payee/currency/contract; source-fact mismatch; disputed or stale decision; competing award/release; concurrent retry; partial authorization balance; crash before/after dispatch; unknown recovery; duplicate/conflicting callback; appealed booked payment with compensation. Need PostgreSQL evidence in addition to SQLite. No live supplier, PSP or merchant credential is required for those tests.

Mandatory latest-state fence: an idempotent replay intentionally returns its original response. A historical adjudicate response may contain a formerly actionable award even after an appeal. C11 must re-resolve the latest verified case under the order/decision lock, require its current decision version/hash and AWAITING_C11_REVIEW state, and reject DISPUTE_HOLD. Never consume an earlier response as a current money instruction. Funds already booked require linked C11 compensation, not history replacement.
